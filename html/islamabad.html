Capital Highlights

Must-See Attractions in Islamabad

Iconic Landmark

Faisal Mosque

Sector E-7, Foothills

Designed by Turkish architect Vedat Dalokay in the shape of a Bedouin desert tent, it is one of the largest and most photographed mosques in South Asia.

National Symbol

Pakistan Monument & Museum

Shakarparian Hills

Petal-shaped granite architectural monument symbolizing the unity of the provinces, featuring wax figures depicting Pakistan's independence history.

Nature & Hiking

Margalla Hills & Daman-e-Koh

Margalla National Park

Pristine Himalayan foothills offering scenic trekking trails (Trail 3 & 5), wild monkey sightings, and panoramic city vistas from Daman-e-Koh and Monal.

Folk Heritage

Lok Virsa Heritage Museum

Garden Avenue

A treasure trove showcasing the living folklore, indigenous textiles, pottery, woodwork, and musical instruments from every region of Pakistan.

Curated Experience

Suggested 3-Day Islamabad Itinerary

Guided tour of the grand Faisal Mosque -> Scenic drive up the Margalla Hills to Daman-e-Koh viewpoint -> Hilltop dinner at Monal or La Montana overlooking the illuminated grid city below.

Visit Pakistan Monument & National History Museum -> Explore Lok Virsa cultural pavilions -> Afternoon walk and sunset boating at Rawal Lake Promenade -> Traditional dinner in Saidpur Heritage Village.

30-minute drive to the UNESCO World Heritage ancient Buddhist university of Taxila (Dharmarajika Stupa & Sirkap) -> Taxila Museum stone carvings -> Return transfer or airport departure.
