Iconic Landmarks

Must-See Attractions in Lahore

From colossal 17th-century mosques to serene terraced Mughal gardens and vibrant heritage bazars.

1673 A.D.

Badshahi Mosque

Walled City, Lahore

Commissioned by Emperor Aurangzeb, this grand red sandstone mosque accommodates over 100,000 worshippers and features magnificent marble inlays.

UNESCO Site

Lahore Fort (Shahi Qila)

Circular Road, Lahore

A sprawling citadel boasting the legendary Shish Mahal (Palace of Mirrors), Picture Wall, Sheesh Mahal, and majestic Alamgiri Gate.

UNESCO Heritage

Shalimar Gardens

GT Road, Lahore

Built by Emperor Shah Jahan in 1642, showcasing three descending terraces, 410 fountains, marble pavilions, and lush ornamental flora.

Cultural Core

Walled City & Delhi Gate

Androon Shehr

Wander through centuries-old spice markets, Shahi Hammam royal bathhouse, and Wazir Khan Mosque known for its intricate fresco tile work.

Curated Experience

Suggested 3-Day Lahore Itinerary

Morning pickup -> Badshahi Mosque & Shahi Qila guided tour -> Afternoon visit to Lahore Museum (Fast-Buddha statue) -> Sunset dinner on the rooftop of Fort View Food Street overlooking illuminated minarets.

Explore Shahi Hammam and Wazir Khan Mosque -> Traditional Lahori brunch (Halwa Puri / Nihari) -> Afternoon drive to Wagah Border for the thrilling Indo-Pak military flag lowering ceremony.

Morning walk through Shalimar Gardens -> Souvenir and handicrafts shopping at Anarkali and Liberty Market Gulberg -> Evening airport transfer or onward tour extension.
