Must-See Attractions in Hunza

Glacial Lake

Attabad Lake

Upper Hunza (Gojal)

Formed in 2010, this mesmerizing turquoise lake stretches over 13 miles, famous for scenic motorized boat safaris and jet ski rides amidst steep canyons.

700 Years Old

Baltit Fort & Karimabad

Karimabad, Central Hunza

UNESCO-restored Tibetan-influenced fortress overlooking the entire valley and Rakaposhi peak, preserving ancient royal woodcraft and royal armor.

Cathedral Peaks

Passu Cones & Glacier

Passu, Upper Hunza

Dramatically serrated jagged mountain pyramids (6,106m) framing the Karakoram Highway, with close hiking access to Passu Glacier.

Panoramic Vista

Eagle's Nest & Duikar

Duikar (2,850m)

The highest viewpoint in the valley offering breathtaking 360-degree sunrise and sunset panoramas over eight 7,000m+ Himalayan & Karakoram summits.

Curated Experience

Suggested 7-Day Hunza Itinerary

Islamabad departure -> Drive along the scenic KKH past the junction point of 3 Great Mountain Ranges (Himalayas, Karakoram, Hindukush) -> Stop at Rakaposhi Viewpoint for tea -> Arrive Karimabad.

Guided tour of 700-yr Baltit Fort & 900-yr Altit Fort -> Taste local traditional Hunza walnut cake & chapshuro -> Sunset photography at Duikar (Eagle's Nest) overlooking the valley.

Attabad Lake boat safari -> Cross thrilling Hussaini Hanging Bridge -> Marvel at Passu Cones -> Drive to Khunjerab Pass (4,693m) - the world's highest paved international border -> Return journey to Islamabad.
