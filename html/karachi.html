Highlights & Icons

Must-See Attractions in Karachi

Coastal Life

Clifton Beach & Sea View

Clifton, Karachi

Famous for energetic seaside sunsets, decorated camel and horse rides along the surf, and oceanfront dining at Do Darya.

National Monument

Mazar-e-Quaid (Mausoleum)

M.A. Jinnah Road

The iconic white marble final resting place of Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah, featuring sprawling manicured gardens and changing of the guard.

Naval History

Pakistan Maritime Museum

Karsaz, Karachi

Explore a real decommissioned Daphne-class submarine (PNS Hangor), naval artifacts, maritime galleries, and artificial lakes.

Colonial Heritage

Empress Market & Saddar

Saddar Heritage District

Victorian Gothic architecture built during British rule in 1889, surrounding bustling spice, dry fruit, and handicraft markets.

Curated Experience

Suggested 3-Day Karachi Itinerary

Morning visit to Mazar-e-Quaid -> Drive past Mohatta Palace and Frere Hall -> Afternoon at Clifton Beach & Sea View -> Fresh seafood dinner directly over the Arabian Sea waves at Do Darya.

Explore Pakistan Maritime Museum and submarine tour -> Drive to 15th-century Chaukhandi Tombs sandstone necropolis -> Evening spice shopping at Empress Market.

Private speed boat cruise to Manora Island & historic lighthouse -> Port Grand waterfront stroll with cultural food pavilions -> Airport departure transfer.
