Transparency & Guest Safety

Policies & Terms of Service

Clear, fair, and transparent guidelines governing bookings, cancellations, environmental protection, and traveler safety.

1. Booking Confirmation & Payment Schedule

• Online reservation through our portal creates an instant provisional hold without immediate charge.

• A 25% deposit is required upon receipt of the formal invoice to guarantee hotel rooms and dedicated transport.

• The remaining balance can be settled 7 days prior to tour commencement or at the start of the trip upon arrival in Pakistan.

2. Cancellation & Refund Policy

Full Flexibility Guarantee:

Cancellations requested 15+ days prior to the departure date receive a 100% full refund (less bank processing charges).

• 14 to 7 Days Prior: 75% refund of total booking value.

• 6 to 3 Days Prior: 50% refund or free date rescheduling within 12 months.

• Under 72 Hours / No-Show: Non-refundable due to non-recoverable hotel locks.

3. Weather, Road Conditions & Force Majeure

Mountain travel in the Karakoram, Himalayas, and Hindukush is subject to unpredictable weather and seasonal road maintenance. In case of road blockages, landslides, or flight cancellations (e.g. Gilgit/Skardu flights), Pakistan Horizons will provide alternative road transit or substitute scenic destinations of equivalent standard without extra service charges.

4. Responsible Tourism & Environmental Code

• Zero Single-Use Plastic Litter: We enforce Leave-No-Trace principles. All trash must be brought back to municipal disposal points.

• Cultural & Religious Respect: Modest clothing is encouraged when visiting historical mosques, shrines, and conservative villages.

• Photography Courtesy: Always ask permission before photographing local residents and women in rural valleys.

5. Privacy & Personal Data Protection

We strictly protect your personal information, passport scans (used solely for NOCs and tourist visa verification), and payment data. We do not sell or share customer data with third-party marketers.
